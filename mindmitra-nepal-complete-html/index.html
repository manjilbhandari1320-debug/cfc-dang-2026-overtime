About MindMitra Nepal

Designed for organizations, built around personal privacy.

An Organization Admin creates temporary accounts for students or employees. Users change their password, accept consent terms and may choose whether counsellors see their identity or an anonymous alias. AI prepares a supportive user summary and a separate professional-review report when permission is provided.

Privacy & Safety

Sensitive information deserves strong protection.

Supabase Authentication, PostgreSQL and Row Level Security separate access by role. Organization Admins cannot read private assessments. Counsellors access only assigned, consented reports and see an alias whenever the client selects anonymous mode.
